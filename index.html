Life Cycle Assessment · TU Munich · 2025

LCA of the Schlossbachschule Bonn school renovation

Team project. Full Life Cycle Assessment of a 3,249 m² school renovation, aligned with EN 15978, EN 15804+A2 and DGNB criteria.

Base-case and optimised models were built to find where the building's carbon comes from and which alternatives reduce it.

Approach

  • Phases A1–A3, B4, B6, C3–C4; functional unit m² gross floor area over 50 years.
  • Models built in OneClick LCA from a BIM (Revit) model, using the Ökobaudat database.
  • Emissions and impact factors evaluated across life-cycle stages, building elements and resource types (Global Warming Potential, Abiotic Depletion Potential).

My contribution

  • Built the base-case and optimised LCA models.
  • Researched English-language standards and database documentation to define scope and methodology.
  • Coordinated project information with architects and engineers across the BIM model.
  • Co-authored the report (methodology, results, recommendations) and prepared summary tables and charts in Excel.

Outcome

  • Identified the main contributors: operational energy, plastic membranes, resilient flooring and ready-mix concrete.
  • Proposed alternatives that reduced GWP by roughly 80%, from 1,773 to 354 kg CO₂e/m².

Climate Protection & Adaptation · TU Munich · 2024

Climate protection and adaptation for Neuperlach, Munich

Chapter author in an 8-person multidisciplinary seminar paper on the sustainable redevelopment of Neuperlach.

Local climate risk was characterised from long-term data, then used to propose measures.

Approach

  • Analysed temperature-trend data (1979–2023) and CO₂-concentration data to characterise local climate risk.
  • Proposed measures with quantified cooling and emissions-reduction benefits.

My contribution

  • Authored the Climate Protection and Climate Change Adaptation chapter.
  • Coordinated with an 8-person team, combining research and written contributions into one consistent paper.

Outcome

  • A climate-risk assessment and a set of adaptation and mitigation measures for the redevelopment area.

Green Infrastructure · TU Munich · 2025

Green infrastructure in Glasgow

Chapter author in a multidisciplinary case study for the seminar “Case Studies of Sustainable Urban Developments and Infrastructure”.

Benchmark analysis of public data and literature on Glasgow's green space, canopy and heat resilience.

Approach

  • Assessed Glasgow's green area ratio (32%) and accessibility.
  • Analysed tree-canopy trends (2013–2023) across 23 wards to identify priority areas for investment.
  • Evaluated pocket parks, green roofs and walls, and green corridors for urban heat and climate resilience.

My contribution

  • Authored the Green Infrastructure chapter, including the research and benchmark analysis.

Outcome

  • Priority areas for investment and an evaluation of interventions for urban heat and resilience.
  • Benchmark analysis
  • Public data
  • Literature research
